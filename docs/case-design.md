# The Blue Hour: authoring and answer contract

All names, locations, objects and records are fictional. No real ANU personal
or security data. The generated scene image is illustrative, not hidden evidence.

## Ground truth
17:20: direct sunlight reaches Blue Hour, a light-sensitive blue glass sculpture.
17:26: registrar Mara Singh signs a precautionary relocation instruction.
17:30: technician Eli Ward carries it out of Gallery 2 in a padded transport case.
The corridor camera reads 17:36 because its clock is six minutes fast.
17:34: the intact object is received in the North Store, rack C.
The object was protected, not stolen. Courier Jules Hart left at 17:10.

## Eight clues
Field folder: empty plinth photograph; card-access log; corridor observation;
route map. Archive folder: camera-clock service slip; condition report;
relocation instruction; receiving receipt. Every clue has full accessible text.

## Required reasoning
Pair the camera observation with the clock slip to establish the real movement
time. The field access record names Eli; the archive instruction authorises the
duty technician. The archive receipt names location N-3, which only the field
route map translates to North Store. Neither folder gives the full answer alone.

## Submission
Choose person, destination, reason and corrected movement time. Cite at least two different published
clues, including one from each folder. Incorrect submissions give targeted hints.
Correct answers also require the four events in chronological order. Every attempt
persists. The full explanation is sent only after the investigation is solved.

## Human evaluation outstanding
Observe two first-time players without explaining the interface. Check that both
contribute an inference, the clock correction is discoverable, and the answer feels
earned. Do not claim this playtest occurred merely because automated tests pass.
