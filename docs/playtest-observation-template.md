## Trace one consequential inference

- Initial explanation, and who proposed it:
- The conflicting/new evidence and who introduced it:
- What the group changed in its explanation:
- Exact observed actions or words supporting that interpretation:
- Alternative explanation (for example, someone already knew or guessed):

This is the main test of “someone else's discovery changes what you think
happened.” Click counts alone do not establish it. Also record any counterexample:
one person may solve everything while others only publish when instructed.

## Ask each participant before group discussion

- What did someone else contribute that you could not have supplied alone?
- Where were you unsure what to do next, and what resolved it?
- What did the 3D scene add? When was the object list easier?
- Did the reading and hints suit this group? What would you remove or change?

For each answer, mark **exact quotation** only if recorded accurately; otherwise
label it **paraphrase**. Do not convert silence into agreement.
