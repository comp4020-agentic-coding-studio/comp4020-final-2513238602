## A short demonstration

1. Explain the intended experience: one person's clue changes the meaning of
   another person's evidence. The eight-clue scope keeps the case understandable.
2. Start solo with a nickname. Read two clues from different folders, publish
   them, and move a chronology event. Refresh to show that the trace remains.
3. If a second browser is available, start a duo investigation and invite it.
   Point out the different folders; publish a clue and observe the partner's
   display update without refreshing. Separate browser profiles are required.
4. Show the contrast between an incorrect explanation's hint and the completed
   story. Avoid revealing the answer before peers try the case themselves.
5. Open PROCESS.md at the offline-recovery correction, follow its commit link,
   and connect the change to CLAUDE.md and the browser regression scenario.

## What is established for C8

The live app performs its core interaction, preserves an investigation, serves
the full README, and has a stack decision, process history and repeatable checks.
Public repository visibility, successful CI and the deployed crit-8 tag are
verified during release. A filename passing check:evidence does not establish
the quality or authenticity of a personal reflection.

## Questions for real players

Observe without coaching: where do they hesitate, can each person explain one
inference, and does correcting the clock feel discoverable? Record actual words
and actions, who observed them, and what changed afterwards. Do not fill in
answers in advance or treat browser automation as a human playtest.
