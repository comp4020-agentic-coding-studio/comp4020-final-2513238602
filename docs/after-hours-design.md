# After Hours: a cooperative investigation

Approved direction, 22 October 2026: the student reported tutor feedback asking
for a friends/family detective game and requested procedural 3D investigation.
This is feedback reported by the student, not evidence of a completed playtest.

## Audience and loop

Two to four people at a table or on a call investigate a fictional community
exhibition. Target duration is 20–30 minutes, not a measured claim. There is no
timer, violence, account requirement or competitive score. A short classic case,
The Blue Hour, keeps its original rules and saved investigations.

Inspect an object → discover a private fact → describe/share it → combine it with
another duty's record → support a deduction → open the next act. All facts have
text equivalents and all object actions have keyboard/touch controls. A viewpoint
or rendering failure must never make a clue unavailable.

## Seven deductions in three acts

1. Cargo: G3 + W2, or G1 + W2. Courier: G2 + W3.
2. Corrected time: M1 + M2. Route: W1 + M4, or W1 + W4.
3. Technician: S2 + S3. Destination: S1 + S4. Purpose: M3 + S2.

Act one opens gallery/workshop, act two adds the watch room, act three adds the
store. Every accepted inference cites actual published supporting facts. Wrong
attempts persist; hints progress through direction, evidence pair, explanation.
Seven supported deductions and six correctly ordered events close the case.

## People and persistence

First player covers scene + objects; second covers archive + routes. Third and
fourth seats take objects and routes respectively. Each required deduction crosses
the two initial perspectives. Teams can explicitly transfer any duty, including
one held by a departed player. A visible persisted handover and version check
prevent silent reassignment. Discovery follows the duty, shared evidence remains
visible to everyone, and no room depends on a special host connection.

New rooms pin after-hours-v1 and a seed. Legacy rooms migrate with blue-hour and
retain their rules. Geometry reuses authored room templates; only decoration is
seeded. Random decoration is not a claim of new mysteries or replay value.

## Sources and evaluation

[Keep Talking and Nobody Explodes](https://keeptalkinggame.com/) motivates
complementary information and conversation. Our pace permits discussion without a
countdown. [React Three Fiber](https://r3f.docs.pmnd.rs/advanced/scaling-performance)
guides resource reuse and demand rendering. Original geometric assets are authored
for this project; no external 3D demo is embedded.

HTTP checks cover permission boundaries, four competing seats, stage gates,
supported/unsupported claims, handovers, stale chronology and restarts. Browser
checks must cover actual rendered shared state and keyboard/mobile alternatives.
Humans still need to judge comprehension, contributions, enjoyment and duration.
